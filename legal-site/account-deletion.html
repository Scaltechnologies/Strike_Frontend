Strike Vendor account deletion

The Strike Vendor app is provided by Strike Technologies Private Limited. You can ask us to delete your Strike Vendor account and the personal information associated with it.

Signing out of the app or uninstalling it does not delete your account.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support.striketechnologies@gmail.com from the email address registered on your vendor account, with the subject “Account deletion request – Strike Vendor”. In the app, the button below prepares this email for you.
  2. Include your registered mobile number and business (shop) name so that we can identify your account.
  3. We may contact you on your registered mobile number or email address to confirm that the request came from you before acting on it.

You do not need the app installed to make a request.

What is deleted and what is kept

When your request is processed, we delete your account and associated personal information, in accordance with applicable law and the Platform’s operational requirements.

Information that must lawfully be retained — such as financial records of card purchases, redemptions, commissions and withdrawals, and records needed for fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other legal obligations — may be retained for the necessary period, with access and use appropriately restricted.

Active memberships held by customers at your store, and any amounts due to or from you, are handled under the STRIKE Terms & Conditions and applicable law.

Questions

We will respond within the period prescribed by applicable law. For questions, contact support.striketechnologies@gmail.com or call 8143604709. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.
